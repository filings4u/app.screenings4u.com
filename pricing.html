SUBSCRIPTION FAQ

Before you subscribe

Do DOT and NON-DOT use one account?

Workforce is the unified software brand. Available DOT and NON-DOT workflows are determined by your account, purchased program access and assigned permissions.

Are tests included in the monthly fee?

A subscription is for software access. Drug testing, screening and fulfillment services may be charged separately unless your plan specifically includes them.

Can C/TPAs manage employer clients?

Workforce includes C/TPA-specific software plans for multi-client administration; available functions depend on the selected tier and entitlements.

How do I get a subscription?

Request the plan you need. Existing customers can use central sign-in; plan activation and checkout should be confirmed before access is granted.

How access works

Plan tier and program entitlement are different: Essential, Professional and Enterprise determine available features, while purchased DOT and/or NON-DOT access determines the applicable programs and authorized portal domains. Individual agency offerings and owner-operator subscriptions can have separate pricing.
